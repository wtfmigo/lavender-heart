# Development photography for the recomposed journey

These 31 unique, self-hosted stock photographs are development composition material. They are not personal photographs or claims about shared memories. Stock portrait subjects are anonymous stand-ins for testing portrait framing; no identity or relationship is implied. All descriptions were checked against the downloaded photographs. No imagery comes from the reference site.

The [official Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license), verified 2026-10-08, permits free commercial and noncommercial download and use. Individual ordinary Unsplash photographs are used within the website, not resold as photographs or made into a competing image service. Original CDN photo IDs are recorded below so each asset can be traced and replaced.

The existing first eight assets are preserved and 23 distinct sources are added. Journey assignment uses each photograph once: Story 09/22/18/21/20/01/06/19; full Memory 29; Gallery 10/02/11/23/12/03/13/24/14/05/15/17/16/04/27/08; Timeline 25/26/28/30/31/07. Story spans portrait, pen closeup, ocean, flowers, fern detail, road, night sky and cinematic mountain scenery. Gallery combines ten vertical anchors with six landscape companions. Timeline includes architecture, city, boat, tree, portrait and lake.

Full derivatives total 5773632 bytes; 600px responsive variants total 2579930 bytes. Full WebP derivatives use quality 80 with bounded 800 x 1067 portraits or 1200 x 800 landscapes. The small variants use quality 78 at 600 x 800 or 600 x 400. Natural source colors are preserved. Photographs are fetched only during preparation; public runtime uses local assets. Captions are empty to avoid repeated placeholder clutter. Focal centers remain 0.5/0.5.

Run from frontend: node scripts/prepare-birthday-media.mjs reproduces all derivatives; pass 9 to start at the first newly added asset. node scripts/inspect-development-media.mjs decodes all full and small files, checks bounds/dimensions and JSON descriptors, checks 31 unique journey sources, checks entry counts/null dates, and creates the contact sheets under docs/evidence/visual-recomposition/. Both contact sheets were visually inspected.

Presentation-only dateLabel values are YOUR YEAR, YOUR DATE and MONTH / DAY. Actual dates and settings stay unchanged. Neutral owner letter/message blocks remain placeholders; the Finale uses the existing generic With love. No invented private prose or personal dates were added.
